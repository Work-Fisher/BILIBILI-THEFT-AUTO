## Processing

- One-shots: mono, cut by hand from onset detection, leading silence removed (threshold -34 dB unless noted), 1 ms fade-in, fade-out over the last 25–30 % (max 0.25 s unless noted), peak -1.2 dBFS.
- Loops (engines, siren, skid, horn hold, water, helicopter, ambience): the cut is one loop plus a cross-fade length; the tail is equal-power (quarter-sine) cross-faded into the head, so the loop point is seamless. Gain to -18 LUFS (engines / mechanical) or -20 LUFS (ambience, stereo) with a -2 / -3 dBFS limiter.
- Engine loops: the native rpm of each loop is an estimate (pitch tracking + the source description), used by js/samples.js to pitch them to the car's rpm. eng_muscle_high is Revving_2 resampled ×1.5.
- Footsteps: single steps picked by onset detection from longer walks / runs, 0.3–0.45 s each.

## Notes on provenance

- Field recordings in public places (beach crowd, LA night traffic, Toronto Chinatown, the first-round street / market beds) carry incidental, unintelligible speech. amb_bar_music is "Distant Dance Club" (DeVern): music muffled through a wall; whether it contains sung words could not be checked by ear.
- gun_* close shots are real firearms on outdoor ranges (Sony PCM-M10, phone, Zoom). gun_far_5 is a real distant shot (Resaural). gun_empty_1 / gun_reload_1 are airsoft foley (first round).
- grunt_* are non-verbal pain grunts (MrFossy, Kodack); no words.
- crash_clank_1 and crash_clank_2 (qubodup) are CC0 sounds built from other CC0 recordings, as their pages say.

Suppressed game variants use the two corresponding gun_pst, gun_smg, gun_rfl or gun_sg recordings and gun_rack clips credited above. Sniper variants use gun_rfl. Each mix has a shorter tail and a filtered transient.
